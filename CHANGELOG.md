## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Extraction test set: criterion for replacing the outdated guide value «12–25 atoms per 45-minute interview» in `evidenz.md` by an order of magnitude taken from the measured runs (about 1.5–2.5 atoms per minute of conversation, around 90–110 for a 45-minute interview with many long turns, not checked on real material). Committed before the runs and before the template change; the change enters `evidenz.md` only if three new blind runs keep the earlier conditions and leave the atom counts of the five interview cases within the range of the six earlier runs
- Extraction test set: confirmation run for the narrower granularity rule – its acceptance relied on a scorer correction made after seeing a result, so three new blind runs over all eleven cases are measured against the same criterion with the corrected scorer from the start. Criterion committed before the first run; if a condition fails, `evidenz.md` is reset, even if the cause is again a scorer error. Result (`runs/2026-10-14-bestaetigung-*`, `runs/2026-10-14-unabhaengig-bestaetigung-*`): all three conditions hold without any scorer change – 172/172 in every run, 6–12 shared required entries in the two-parent interview, 71/71 with 1–2 rule violations per run, none a split turn. The rule stays. Two batches with byte-identical bundles differ more than the runs within one batch on the long interviews (two-parent interview 87–92 against 102–106 atoms, `signal` mismatches 93 against 64), so the earlier reading that the narrower rule keeps less of the `signal` gain does not hold
- Extraction test set: `compare` gains a section «Körnung» for cases with `shared_atoms` – per run the required entries that share an atom with another found required entry, the found required entries and the atom count. It makes the granularity criterion reproducible from the repository (the numbers of the earlier runs are unchanged: 37–42 of 61 with the current template in the two-parent interview, 6–12 of 63 with the rejected rule); the two comparisons over the independent cases are regenerated with it. Measurement run for a narrower granularity rule – only for interviews, short turns of up to three sentences stay one atom – with the criterion committed before the first run. Result over three blind runs on all eleven cases (`runs/2026-10-14-eng-*`, `runs/2026-10-14-unabhaengig-eng-*`, comparisons `runs/2026-10-14-eng-vergleich.md` and `runs/2026-10-14-unabhaengig-eng-vergleich.md`): all 172 required entries of the independent cases in every run, 14–16 shared required entries in the two-parent interview (before 37–42), and in the eight own cases 71/71, field accuracy ≥ 99.6 % and one rule violation per run, none of them a split turn. All three conditions hold, so the rule stays in `evidenz.md`. Disclosed deviation: with the scorer as it stood when the criterion was fixed, run 1 counts 70/71 and condition 3 fails – the assignment error fixed below, found in exactly that run; the correction changes no earlier result. The narrower rule keeps less of the broader one's gain: field accuracy against the independent gold 97 % (before 96 %, broader rule 98 %), 87–92 atoms in the two-parent interview (41–47, broader rule 91–102)
- Extraction test set: measurement run for a granularity rule in the extraction template («one atom per statement, not per sentence and not per turn»), with the acceptance criterion committed before the first run. Three blind runs over all eleven cases (`runs/2026-10-14-koernung-*`, `runs/2026-10-14-unabhaengig-koernung-*`, comparisons in `runs/2026-10-14-koernung-vergleich.md` and `runs/2026-10-14-unabhaengig-koernung-vergleich.md`). On long turns the rule works: every run finds all 172 required entries of the independent cases (before 170), only 6–12 of the 63 required entries in the two-parent interview share an atom (before 37–42), and field accuracy against the independent gold rises from 96 to 98 % because each atom carries its own `signal`. But it also splits two-sentence turns that the own cases treat as one statement and raises atom counts for other source kinds: 6, 6 and 4 rule violations against 0 before, where the criterion allowed a mean of 2. The change is rejected; `evidenz.md` is unchanged. Next candidate: the same rule limited to long interview turns, with its own criterion
- Extraction test set: three blind runs each with the old and the current template on the independent cases (`runs/2026-10-14-unabhaengig-*`). Coverage does not separate the templates even against an independent gold (98–100 % for both), but granularity does: the current template merges long turns (41–47 atoms against 70–83 in the two-parent interview) and misses two statements there in every run. Field accuracy against the independent gold is 96–97 % for both – the disagreement of two judgements on `signal` and emotion, with the extraction rating more sharply. A template change «one atom per statement, also in long turns» is the next candidate. Gold fragments that contained school names are cut like person names, since every run had replaced them
- Extraction test set: independent cases – transcripts written by a subagent that never saw `evidenz.md`, gold set by a second subagent that knew only the atom model, not the prompt template. Scorer: `shared_atoms` in `expected.json` lets one atom cover several gold entries, so a gold that splits long turns more finely does not count granularity as a gap
- Extraction test set: three harder synthetic cases – a longer interview (`interview-hort`: deadline next to the experienced duration with a correction mid-sentence, third parties acting for the persona, costs with their period and whether they were known in advance, feelings of others), a parent survey (`befragung`: n of sub-groups, a negatively worded item above 60 % agreement, free texts only where they show what the numbers do not) and a complaint (`beschwerde`: power of attorney, Swiss thousands separator, irony, two contradictions). Scorer: units as a list of equivalent spellings, `text_has` for details the text must carry, `prompts --evidenz` for an older template and `--bundle` for everything a run may see, `compare` for several runs side by side and per template. Three blind runs each with the template before and after the revision of 2026-10-08 (`runs/2026-10-14-*`, comparison in `runs/2026-10-14-vergleich.md`): the new cases now separate the two templates on field accuracy (96 % vs 100 %, the old one misses `value`, `unit` and `kind` exactly where the revision added rules) and on rules (82–83 vs 0 violations), but not on coverage – every run finds all 71 required atoms. The current template exhausts the test set; it detects regressions, not further improvement. Material not written for the test is still needed
- Schema: source kind `regulation` for public legal acts – laws, ordinances, regulations, tariff and fee schedules (viewer: «Erlass» / «Acte normatif»). Like `internal_document` it says what should apply, not what users experience, and does not count as a user source for L011; `internal_document` keeps process descriptions, target processes and maps taken over. The fee schedule of `examples/musikschule/` uses it. Schema stays 1.0 (additive)
- Audit report: line «Breakdowns an Übergängen: n von m · Übergänge: k von s Schritten» under «Evidenzlage» – how many breakdowns lie at a step marked `is_transition`, next to the share of transitions among all steps as a base rate (finding H14 of the music-school example). In the three examples 75–80 % of the steps are transitions, so «all breakdowns at a transition» on its own proves little
- Schema: optional `step.costs[]` (ADR-0007, accepted) – cost items the persona bears, at the step where they fall due: `label`, `amount` with optional `min`/`max` (an evidenced range, e.g. with a fee reduction), `currency` (default CHF), required `period` (`once`, `month`, `semester`, `year`), `paid_to` as a role, `basis` and required `evidence_refs`. No totals – costs in a journey are often alternatives (rent or buy) or conditional; whether users knew the costs in advance stays a KPI per cost type, not a field. Lint L004 (ERROR): contradictory range (min > amount or amount > max); lint L103 (INFO): costs backed only by `assumed` evidence such as a fee schedule, which says what should apply, not what was paid. Viewer: row «Kosten» / «Coûts» under «Macht» with amount, period, range, payee and evidence drill-down, hidden by the primary-evidence filter when only assumptions back it. Audit report: section «Kosten» / «Coûts» with a table per step. Cost evidence counts as referenced (L001, L100). Examples: five entries in the music school, the building fee in the building permit; all example viewers re-rendered. Skill: the cost pattern in `SKILL.md` now uses the field. Schema stays 1.0 (additive). Stage 2 of #15, finding H8
- Schema: optional `pain_point.caused_by` (ADR-0008, accepted) – the IDs of the pain points this one follows from: without them it would not exist. One concept for avoidable contacts (failure demand) and consequential costs, also across phases and offices; replaces the `step.avoidable` option of #49. The cause is always a pain point in the same or an earlier step, never a back-stage note; the evidence for the link sits with the consequence. Lints: `L001.caused_by` / `L001.caused_by_self` (ERROR) for an unknown cause or a self-reference, L005 (ERROR) for a cause that comes after its consequence, `L005.cycle` for causes in a circle within a step, L065 (INFO) for an opportunity that links only consequences and addresses none of their causes, direct or indirect. Viewer: «Folge von ‹step›» / «Conséquence de ‹étape›» under the pain point in the journey and process layers, jumping to the cause; the cause shows how many consequences it has. Audit report: line «Ursachenketten» / «Chaînes de causes» when there are links. Examples: four links in the music school, one in the building permit. Skill: the pattern for avoidable steps uses the field; two review questions. L061 stays as it is: an opportunity at the cause covers a severe consequence only if it lists it in `linked_pain_points` (decision recorded in ADR-0008). Schema stays 1.0 (additive). Finding H9 from the music school example
- Third worked example `examples/musikschule/`: enrolment at a music school from the parents' perspective (waiting list, fee reduction requested from the municipality, lessons arranged via the teacher's messenger, cancellation that only counts in writing). Avoidable contacts whose cause lies phases earlier at another office need a link (recommendation: `pain_point.caused_by` instead of `step.avoidable`, #49). Deadlines the persona must meet, the prescribed vs. the used channel, the beneficiary's feelings and the narrow description of `counterpart` get a skill pattern or review question first. Schema and lints are unchanged. `docs/konzept.md`, section «Evaluation», gains an overview of what the model lacked across all examples

Skill: warning signs for a target concept in `references/diagnose.md`, the audit transfer sets `to_be` as an inferred statement with a changelog entry, a review question and the extra condition for «robust» in `review-fragen.md`. ### Fixed
- Extraction test set: the scorer assigned gold entries to atoms greedily in gold order, so an atom that also fit an earlier entry could be taken although that entry had its own atom – the later entry then counted as missed. Example from a run of the narrower granularity rule: the atom «8 von 20 Anfragen … Unterlagen» names «3 Telefon» and the ticket «A-12», so it also fit the phone metric (12 calls) and was taken from `kategorie-unterlagen`. The assignment now first takes a free atom as before and otherwise re-routes an occupied one when its entry gets another matching atom. All recorded runs and comparisons score unchanged; a test covers the case
- Viewer: singular and plural for counts in the header, the hidden-items notes and the heatmap (was «1 Warnungen», «1 ohne Primärevidenz ausgeblendet»)
- Viewer: the persona overlay in the emotion curve no longer drops data silently – phases without data or without emotion evidence of the other persona are labelled, phases without a matching ID are listed, and the legend notes that positions within a phase are approximate (#9)
- Viewer: the persona badge in the header wraps instead of overflowing; long persona names pushed the page to 481 px at a 390 px viewport

### Security
- `/eval/` is git-ignored: raw material and ground truth for the evaluation stay local; only the comparison report (under `docs/evaluation/`) is committed
