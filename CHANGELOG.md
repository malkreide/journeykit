## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Schema: optional `step.costs[]` (ADR-0007, accepted) – cost items the persona bears, at the step where they fall due: `label`, `amount` with optional `min`/`max` (an evidenced range, e.g. with a fee reduction), `currency` (default CHF), required `period` (`once`, `month`, `semester`, `year`), `paid_to` as a role, `basis` and required `evidence_refs`. No totals – costs in a journey are often alternatives (rent or buy) or conditional; whether users knew the costs in advance stays a KPI per cost type, not a field. Lint L004 (ERROR): contradictory range (min > amount or amount > max); lint L103 (INFO): costs backed only by `assumed` evidence such as a fee schedule, which says what should apply, not what was paid. Viewer: row «Kosten» / «Coûts» under «Macht» with amount, period, range, payee and evidence drill-down, hidden by the primary-evidence filter when only assumptions back it. Audit report: section «Kosten» / «Coûts» with a table per step. Cost evidence counts as referenced (L001, L100). Examples: five entries in the music school, the building fee in the building permit; all example viewers re-rendered. Skill: the cost pattern in `SKILL.md` now uses the field. Schema stays 1.0 (additive). Stage 2 of #15, finding H8
- Schema: optional `pain_point.caused_by` (ADR-0008, accepted) – the IDs of the pain points this one follows from: without them it would not exist. One concept for avoidable contacts (failure demand) and consequential costs, also across phases and offices; replaces the `step.avoidable` option of #49. The cause is always a pain point in the same or an earlier step, never a back-stage note; the evidence for the link sits with the consequence. Lints: `L001.caused_by` / `L001.caused_by_self` (ERROR) for an unknown cause or a self-reference, L005 (ERROR) for a cause that comes after its consequence, `L005.cycle` for causes in a circle within a step, L065 (INFO) for an opportunity that links only consequences and addresses none of their causes, direct or indirect. Viewer: «Folge von ‹step›» / «Conséquence de ‹étape›» under the pain point in the journey and process layers, jumping to the cause; the cause shows how many consequences it has. Audit report: line «Ursachenketten» / «Chaînes de causes» when there are links. Examples: four links in the music school, one in the building permit. Skill: the pattern for avoidable steps uses the field; two review questions. Whether an opportunity at the cause satisfies L061 for its consequences stays open. Schema stays 1.0 (additive). Finding H9 from the music school example
- Third worked example `examples/musikschule/`: enrolment at a music school from the parents' perspective (waiting list, fee reduction requested from the municipality, lessons arranged via the teacher's messenger, cancellation that only counts in writing). Synthetic raw inputs: 3 interviews, 30 inquiries, case statistics, a parent survey, web analytics, the fee schedule, a process document and workshop notes; 6 phases, 15 steps, 4 breakdowns – all at a change of channel or office – and 140 atoms, 0 errors and 0 warnings. The README documents eight transferability hypotheses (H8–H15, continuing H1–H7 of the building permit). Costs that recur, are reduced or go to third parties do not fit the pattern from #15 any more (recommendation: `step.costs[]`, with «known in advance» as a KPI rather than a field). Avoidable contacts whose cause lies phases earlier at another office need a link (recommendation: `pain_point.caused_by` instead of `step.avoidable`, #49). Deadlines the persona must meet, the prescribed vs. the used channel, the beneficiary's feelings and the narrow description of `counterpart` get a skill pattern or review question first. Schema and lints are unchanged. `docs/konzept.md`, section «Evaluation», gains an overview of what the model lacked across all examples
- Lint L054 (INFO): no trigger (`scenario.trigger`) – the counterpart of L053 for the start of the journey; the hint asks whether the journey begins where the persona begins (life event, a hint from acquaintances) rather than at the first contact with the administration. Two review questions under «Inside-Out»: where the journey really begins, and which pain points are only known because users name them while latent needs would need observation (`observed`). Prompted by an HBS Online article on journey mapping («a journey map challenges your assumptions about when the journey truly begins and ends»); the rest of the article was already covered
- Skill: pattern for avoidable steps (failure demand – a follow-up call or second attempt needed only because a step before failed) without a schema field: the step gets a `friction` pain point saying why it is avoidable, the cause belongs to the step before (a breakdown, or an open question with `step_ref` when not evidenced), the opportunity addresses the cause, KPI «Anteil vermeidbarer Kontakte» (`public_value`, leading) in `kpi-uebersetzung.md`. Audit mode: an avoidability mark on the original becomes an atom and a `friction` pain point, the cause an open question. Review question «Who calls although they would not need to?» points to the pattern. A schema field (`step.avoidable`) only when a third example needs it (#49)
- Schema: optional `opportunity.step_ref` – the step an opportunity addresses, more precise than `phase_ref`. L001 reports an unknown step and a step outside the given `phase_ref`; L064 and the proposed verdict count `step_ref` as an anchor; story map and viewer place the opportunity in its step's phase, and the viewer's opportunity card names the step with a jump to the journey layer. Skill, audit mode: an idea the original assigns to a column goes into `step_ref` instead of an invented phase. Schema stays 1.0 (additive) (#50)
- Schema: optional `scenario.perspective` (`as_is` – the path as users experience it today, the default – or `to_be` – a target concept describing how the path is meant to work). A target concept transferred into the model looked like an as-is journey without friction; now the viewer shows a badge «Soll-Konzept» / «Concept cible», L020 reports the missing breakdowns as INFO with its own message (which of today's breakdowns the concept is meant to fix), and the proposed verdict is never «robust»: the audit report names the target concept under «Diagnose» and gives it its own verdict sentence (a target concept cannot be evidenced by users, only justified by an as-is journey – not «not yet evidenced»). Skill: warning signs for a target concept in `references/diagnose.md`, the audit transfer sets `to_be` as an inferred statement with a changelog entry, a review question and the extra condition for «robust» in `review-fragen.md`. ADR-0006 (accepted). Schema stays 1.0 (additive) (#41)
- Lint L010, variant `L010.empty` (WARN, INFO for `status: hypothesis`): a journey without a single experience statement (thoughts, feelings, pain points) – before, L010 measured only a share and stayed silent when there was nothing to measure. The audit report always prints the experience line («Erlebnis-Aussagen: keine»), and the proposed verdict names «no experience statement» instead of «0% primary evidence» (#42)
- Lint L035 (INFO): neither `meta.created` nor `meta.updated` – the age of the journey is unknown, the first question for a map taken over from elsewhere (#31)
- Lint L053 (INFO): no end state (`scenario.end_state`) – a journey that stops before the persona's goal is reached hides either a breakdown or a scope boundary; matching review question under «Happy Path» (#29)
- Lint L064 (WARN, INFO for `status: hypothesis`): opportunities linked to neither a pain point (`linked_pain_points`) nor a phase (`phase_ref`) – typical for an idea band at the foot of a workshop map; without the link nobody can check which problem an opportunity solves (#27)
- Audit report: sections «Diagnose» (`meta.diagnosis` with alternatives and back-stage reference, or a note that it is missing), «Urteil (Vorschlag)» and «Nächste Schritte» before the evidence (#26). The verdict is proposed from the model by `model.propose_verdict` along the criteria in `review-fragen.md` – poster names what is missing, hypothesis names the failed conditions – and is marked as a proposal for the review to confirm. Next steps are the three most severe findings, one per anti-pattern, with their hint; a hypothesis without a research plan gets the plan as first step. German and French texts in `export_texts.py`
- Schema: `open_questions.proposed_method` accepts `source_request` (ask the authors or the responsible office for sources or raw material – the first question of most audits). Schema stays 1.0 (additive)
- Test set for the extraction prompt (`skill/user-journey/evals/extraction/`): five synthetic cases (interview, inquiry export, process document, case statistics, team workshop), each with source, template values and rules (`case.json`), gold atoms (`expected.json`) and a hand-made reference extraction. `scripts/eval_extraction.py` fills the template straight from `evidenz.md` and scores an extraction for required atoms found, field accuracy and rule violations; CI checks that every reference scores perfectly and that typical errors are caught. Two blind runs are recorded under `runs/` (48 rule violations with the old template, 0 with the revised one)
- Command line in French: messages («écrit : …», schema errors, `validate`), help texts and the `journeykit new` scaffold (`--lang` sets `meta.language` and French placeholders). Language order for every command: `--lang`, then `meta.language` of the journey, then the environment variable `JOURNEYKIT_LANG`, then German; help follows `--lang` or `JOURNEYKIT_LANG`. Texts live in `cli_texts.py`; German messages are unchanged, the German help gains the language note and the new `--lang` options. Texts generated by argparse itself and JSON Schema error details stay English, as before
- Exports and diff report in French: `journeykit export --lang de|fr` and `journeykit diff --lang de|fr` (default from `meta.language`, for `diff` of the new version) for the story map, the opportunities matrix, the audit report and the diff report, including the anti-pattern headings and – via `lint_journey(lang=…)` – the findings. Texts live in `export_texts.py`; German output is unchanged. The actions CSV keeps its technical column names and needs no language. `tests/test_export_texts.py` checks key and placeholder parity, that every key used in `export.py` and `diff.py` exists and that a French export contains no German template text
- Lint messages and hints in French: `journeykit lint --lang de|fr`, default from `meta.language`; `render` uses the interface language for the audit tab. Messages and hints moved from `lint.py` into the catalogue `lint_messages.py` (one entry per message ID, identical placeholders in both languages); German output is unchanged. `tests/test_lint_messages.py` checks the catalogue and, via the AST, that every `c.add` call in `lint.py` fills exactly the placeholders of its message
- Viewer interface in French: `journeykit render --lang de|fr`, default from `meta.language` of the first journey (German if there is no interface for that language). All interface texts live in one JSON block (`jk-i18n`) with identical keys per language; journey content is not translated. `tests/test_viewer_i18n.py` checks key and placeholder parity, that every key used in the code exists and that every schema enum value has a label in each language
- `python -m journeykit` entry point for environments where the console script is not on PATH (Windows user installs)
- `tests/test_examples.py`: contract for every example under `examples/*/` – schema-valid, `meta.synthetic: true`, no lint errors, `journey.json` without warnings, every quote atom verbatim in its source file, no contact data in raw inputs. New examples are picked up without extra code
- Second worked example `examples/baubewilligung/`: building permit for a heat pump, applicant and neighbour as two personas, synthetic raw inputs (3 interviews, 30 inquiries, case statistics, web analytics, process document, workshop notes); README documents seven transferability hypotheses and what the example showed
- ADR-0005 (accepted): optional `step.performed_by` (`kind` persona/shared/intermediary, `role`, `mandate` formal/informal) for steps carried out by intermediaries on the persona's behalf – finding H1 from the building-permit example. Schema stays 1.0 (additive)
- Schema: channel `publication` (official publication, notice board, building profile) and source kind `case_records` (case-management statistics, counted as a user source for L011) – findings H7 and «missing source kind» from the building-permit example

### Changed
- Skill, audit mode: a quote the original attributes to users becomes `kind: quote` (class `assumed`, source the map) instead of `document_excerpt`. The audit report counts such second-hand quotes – quotes from a source that is not a user source – under «Evidenzlage» and puts the request for the raw material first under «Nächste Schritte», citing the `source_request` open question or asking to add one. Review questions: hidden breakdowns also in quotes, and a feeling value that stands for several contradicting voices (#48)
- Audit report and viewer (tab «Prüfung») show where an open question belongs: the step (`step_ref`), otherwise the phase (`phase_ref`), as «name»: before the text; in the viewer a click jumps to the step or phase in the journey layer. Before, the reference was checked by L001 but shown nowhere (#51)
- Viewer: when two or more files carry the same persona name (variants of the same request – same persona, different path), the persona selector and the emotion-curve overlay label them «persona – scenario» from `scenario.title`; before, both entries read the same. The header controls no longer push the page wider than a 390 px viewport when an entry is long. Skill (`references/diagnose.md`): variants of the same request get one file each, with the same phase IDs where the paths coincide (#43)
- Skill, audit mode: the date of the original goes into `meta.created` only when the original states it; a date found only in file metadata goes into `sources[].date` with a note, so L035 still reports the unknown age. Authorship from file metadata is never taken over (it is often a personal name); only a role or office the original names. The L035 hint mentions the date of the original (#44)
- Example `kindergarteneintritt`: steps «Eingewöhnung mit reduziertem Stundenplan» (the after-school care joins) and «Alltag und Informationsfluss finden» (it drops out again) marked `is_transition` – the changes of counterpart L023 reports since #28
- Example `baubewilligung`: step «Unterlagen zusammenstellen» marked `is_transition` – the hand-over from the building advice service to the installer that L023 now reports (#28)
- Lint L023 also reports a change of `counterpart` within the same channel that is not marked `is_transition` (message variant `L023.counterpart`, INFO) – e.g. a link from one authority's website to another's; a step with both a channel and a counterpart change gets one finding. Skill: when the responsible body changes, `counterpart` names the new body and the step where it first appears carries `is_transition: true`, no «A → B» (#28)
- Skill: two review questions – a feeling value that contradicts what happens in the step, and a failure that only the activity text mentions; in audit mode such hidden breakdowns become open questions with `step_ref` instead of pain points the original never had (#30)
- Skill, audit mode: the transfer of an existing map keeps track of where each statement comes from (#25, variant A). The map is a source `internal_document` with class `assumed`; every transferred passage becomes a `document_excerpt` atom quoted verbatim with a locator on the map's grid; statements the audit infers point to the atom they were inferred from and are named in the `changelog`; additions live only in `open_questions`. Studies the map merely mentions are not registered as sources. A `changelog` entry listing what the transfer lost or interpreted is required. Replaces «missing evidence is empty `evidence_refs`»
- Skill: verdict criteria in `references/review-fragen.md` («Übergabe-Satz») are mutually exclusive and checked in order – poster if owner, review cycle or one opportunity linked to a pain point or phase is missing; robust if at least half of the experience statements carry primary evidence, the status is not `hypothesis` and every breakdown has a recovery path; hypothesis otherwise (#25)
- Skill: extraction prompt in `references/evidenz.md` revised after the first test-set run – passes the source id (`{source_id}`), one atom per statement rather than per sentence, never the same statement twice, durations in interviews stay quotes with `value` and `unit`, names in quotes replaced by «[Name]», allowed `channel_hint` values listed; inquiry exports get one metric per category and per channel
- `CLAUDE.md`: conventions for the four text catalogues (viewer, lint, exports, CLI) in one table; open items reordered by priority, completed items condensed into one line
- Skill: pattern for costs and fees without a schema field – metric atom in CHF, linked from the step, pain point when costs come as a surprise; extraction rule for fee schedules, invoices and interview statements; KPI «share who knew the costs in advance» (#15, stage 1)
- Skill: review question «breakdown or a legal remedy that works?» (Einsprache, Rekurs), with a note in the synthesis step and the public-administration context of `methodik.md` – finding H4 (#14)
- `journeykit lint` text output prints each rule's hint only once per run
- CI validates and lints every example directory instead of a hard-coded path

### Fixed
- Viewer: singular and plural for counts in the header, the hidden-items notes and the heatmap (was «1 Warnungen», «1 ohne Primärevidenz ausgeblendet»)
- Viewer: the persona overlay in the emotion curve no longer drops data silently – phases without data or without emotion evidence of the other persona are labelled, phases without a matching ID are listed, and the legend notes that positions within a phase are approximate (#9)
- Viewer: the persona badge in the header wraps instead of overflowing; long persona names pushed the page to 481 px at a 390 px viewport

### Security
- `/eval/` is git-ignored: raw material and ground truth for the evaluation stay local; only the comparison report (under `docs/evaluation/`) is committed

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-05

### Added
- Journey model as JSON Schema 2020-12 (`schema_version` 1.0): persona, scenario, phases, steps, pain points (friction/breakdown), edge cases, recovery paths, KPIs, opportunities with stories, sources with PII status, evidence atoms with evidence class, open questions, changelog
- Methodological lints L001–L102 for the five journey-mapping pitfalls plus blueprint smell, privacy, KPI translation and evidence quality
- Self-contained interactive viewer with five layers (journey, process and failure paths, evidence heatmap, opportunities matrix, audit), evidence drill-down, primary-evidence filter, multi-persona overlay, light/dark, print
- CLI `journeykit`: validate, lint, render, export (storymap, actions, opportunities, audit), diff, new, schema
- Claude skill `user-journey` with modes Synthesis, Workshop and Audit, and references on diagnosis, evidence, methodology, KPI translation and review questions
- Worked synthetic example «Kindergarteneintritt aus Elternsicht» with raw inputs, two personas and a workshop-hypothesis version for diffing
- Concept paper and ADRs 0001–0004
